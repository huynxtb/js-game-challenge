## Cogwork Corekeeper

Welcome, Corekeeper! You are entrusted with the monumental task of maintaining the colossal 'Titan of Gears' – an ancient, subterranean automaton. Its intricate clockwork heart is unstable, and if left unattended, it threatens a catastrophic geological collapse. Your mission is to keep its delicate systems operational by precisely managing its internal parameters for a set number of turns, ultimately stabilizing the Titan and averting disaster.

### Objective

Survive for **50 turns** by managing the Titan's core systems. If you succeed, the Titan will stabilize, and disaster will be averted. If any system fails critically, the Titan will be lost, and so will the world above.

### Game Mechanics

The game is turn-based. Each turn represents a cycle of the Titan's internal processes. You must make strategic decisions to manage four key operational parameters:

*   **Pressure (PSI):** Represents the internal pressure of the Titan's steam-driven mechanisms.
    *   **Ideal Range:** 40-60 PSI.
    *   **Danger Zones:** Below 20 PSI (Stall Risk) or Above 80 PSI (Explosion Risk).
    *   **Critical Limits:** 0 PSI (Catastrophic Stall - Game Over) or 100 PSI (Explosion - Game Over).
*   **Lubricant (Oil):** The essential fluid for reducing friction and maintaining gear health.
    *   **Usage:** Consumed by various actions and passively depletes.
    *   **Critical Limit:** 0 Oil (Cannot perform oil-requiring actions - Game Over if needed).
*   **Gear Integrity (Core Gear):** The durability of the Titan's primary drive gear, crucial for its function.
    *   **Degradation:** Decreases faster if Pressure or Heat are outside ideal ranges.
    *   **Critical Limit:** 0 Gear Integrity (Catastrophic Failure - Game Over).
*   **Heat (Celsius):** The internal temperature generated by the Titan's operations.
    *   **Ideal Range:** Below 50°C.
    *   **Danger Zone:** Above 70°C.
    *   **Critical Limit:** 100°C (Meltdown - Game Over).
*   **Turns Remaining:** The countdown to the Titan's full stabilization.

### Player Actions

After each action, the game state updates, and random events may occur. You can perform one action per turn, except for 'Monitor Systems'.

*   **'1' - Adjust Pressure:**
    *   **'1a' - Increase PSI:** Increases PSI by 10-20. Consumes 5 Oil. Increases Heat by 5.
    *   **'1b' - Decrease PSI:** Decreases PSI by 10-20. Reduces Heat by 5 (less effective if Heat is already low).
*   **'2' - Apply Lubricant:**
    *   **'2a' - Apply Oil (Heavy):** Consumes 20 Oil. Repairs 30 Gear Integrity. Reduces Heat by 10.
    *   **'2b' - Apply Oil (Light):** Consumes 10 Oil. Repairs 15 Gear Integrity. Reduces Heat by 5.
*   **'3' - Emergency Vent Heat:** Reduces Heat by 20. Decreases PSI by 15. Consumes 5 Oil.
*   **'4' - Monitor Systems:** Displays current stats. Does not consume a turn.

### Turn Progression Logic

After your action, the following occurs:

1.  **Passive Pressure Fluctuation:** PSI subtly shifts.
2.  **Passive Gear Degradation:** Core Gear Integrity decreases if Pressure or Heat are outside ideal ranges.
3.  **Passive Oil Depletion:** Minor leaks consume Oil.
4.  **Passive Heat Increase:** Heat rises if Pressure is high or Gear Integrity is low.
5.  **Random Events:** Every 3-5 turns, a random event can occur (e.g., Pressure Surge, Oil Leak, Gear Grind, or a rare positive event).
6.  **Turns Remaining** decrements by 1.

### Win Condition

Successfully survive 50 turns (`Turns Remaining` reaches 0).

### Loss Conditions

*   `Core Gear Integrity` drops to 0.
*   `Pressure (PSI)` reaches 100 (explosion) or drops to 0 (catastrophic stall).
*   `Heat (Celsius)` reaches 100 (meltdown).
*   Attempting an action that requires Oil when `Oil` is 0.

### Setup and Running the Game

1.  **Save the code:** Copy the entire JavaScript code provided and save it into a single file named `cogwork_corekeeper.js`.
2.  **Open your terminal or command prompt.**
3.  **Navigate to the directory** where you saved the `cogwork_corekeeper.js` file.
4.  **Run the game** using Node.js:
    
    node cogwork_corekeeper.js
    
5.  Follow the on-screen prompts to play the game.

**To quit the game at any time, type `exit` and press Enter.**
